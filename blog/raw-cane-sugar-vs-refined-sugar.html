A better treat starts before the recipe begins. At CaneCreme, raw cane sugar is part of how we build flavour: warm, rounded, and familiar without making the treat feel flat or overly sweet.

Why raw cane sugar

Raw cane sugar is less processed than refined white sugar and keeps a more natural cane character. That matters in cookies, bites, tea cakes, makhana mixes, and hampers because sweetness should support the ingredient, not cover it.

Taste and texture

Raw cane sugar brings a gentle caramel note. It pairs beautifully with cocoa, dry fruits, oats, millets, and spices, which is why it works across CaneCreme snacks instead of belonging to only one product line.

How CaneCreme uses it

We use raw cane sugar thoughtfully, alongside ingredients people recognize. The goal is not to make treats medicinal; it is to make indulgence feel lighter, cleaner, and more intentional.

FAQ

Is raw cane sugar the same as refined white sugar?

No. Raw cane sugar is less processed and has more natural cane character.

Are CaneCreme treats sugar-free?

No. They are made with raw cane sugar and should still be enjoyed in moderation.
